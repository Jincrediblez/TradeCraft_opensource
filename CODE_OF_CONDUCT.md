# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in TradeCraft a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, evidence-oriented, and considerate of privacy. Do not publish another person's financial, account, or contact information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
